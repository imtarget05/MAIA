## Section 1: IT Help Desk
Contact: it-help@company.com | ext 202 | Slack #it-help | Jira Service Desk. Response time: P1 within 1 hour, P2 within 4 hours.

## Section 2: Laptop Issues
If laptop is broken, slow, or not booting, contact IT Help Desk. For hardware failure, IT will provide a temporary device within 4 hours. For software issues, remote support via TeamViewer is available.

## Section 3: Software Installation
Software requests must be approved by IT Security. Use Service Desk to request.
