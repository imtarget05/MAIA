# Expense Policy v2.0 — Finance

## Section 1: Reimbursable Expenses
Travel, meals with clients (up to 1M VND), office supplies, and training courses are reimbursable with receipts.

## Section 2: Submission Process
Submit expenses via Finance portal within 30 days. Attach receipts as PDF. Manager approval required for amounts >5M VND.

## Section 3: Travel Expenses
Flights must be economy class for domestic travel. Hotels up to 2M VND/night require pre-approval.

## Section 4: Timeline
Reimbursements are processed within 10 business days after approval.

Finance Help: finance@company.com | ext 303
