# Onboarding Guide v1.5 — People & Culture

## Section 1: First Day
Collect laptop from IT Help Desk (ext 202), setup SSO, join Slack workspace, meet buddy.

## Section 2: Accounts
Accounts provisioned automatically: Email, Slack, Jira, Confluence, GitLab. VPN access requires separate request.

## Section 3: Training
Complete mandatory security training within first week via LMS.

## Section 4: Policies to Read
HR Policy, Security Policy v4.2, Leave Policy, Expense Policy, VPN Guide.

## Section 5: Support
Ask MAIA for any onboarding questions, or contact onboarding@company.com
