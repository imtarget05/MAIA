# IT Security Policy v4.2 — IT Department

## Section 1: Password Policy
Passwords must be at least 12 characters with mixed case, numbers, and symbols. Rotate every 90 days. Use company SSO where possible.

## Section 2: Device Management
All company laptops must have disk encryption and MDM installed. Lost or stolen devices must be reported immediately.

## Section 7.1: Lost Device Procedure
If an employee loses a company laptop or mobile device, they must report to IT Help Desk within 1 hour of discovery. Contact: it-help@company.com | ext 202 | Slack #it-help. IT will remotely lock the device, revoke access tokens, and issue a replacement within 2 business days. Failure to report within 1 hour may result in security review.

## Section 8: VPN Access
VPN is required for remote access to internal services (Confluence, Jira, GitLab). Request VPN via MAIA or IT portal. Approval is automatic for employees with active status.

## Section 9: Incident Reporting
Security incidents should be reported to security@company.com immediately.
