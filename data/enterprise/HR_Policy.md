# HR Policy — Enterprise Employee Handbook v3.1

## Section 1: Working Hours
Standard working hours are 09:00-18:00 Monday-Friday. Flexible arrangement requires manager approval.

## Section 2: Leave Policy Summary
All full-time employees are entitled to 12 annual leave days per calendar year. Leave requests must be submitted via HR portal at least 3 days in advance for 1-3 days leave, and 7 days in advance for >3 days leave. Manager approval is required.

## Section 3: Leave Request Process (Detailed)
To request leave, employees submit a leave request with start date, end date, and number of days. The system checks remaining balance and creates a Leave Request ID (format LV-YYYYMMDD-XXX). The request is routed to the direct manager for approval. Approved leave is deducted from the annual balance.

## Section 4: Benefits
Health insurance covers inpatient and outpatient treatment. Annual health check is provided in Q4.
